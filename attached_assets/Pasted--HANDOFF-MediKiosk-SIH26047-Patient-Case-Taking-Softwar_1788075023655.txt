# 1. PROJECT

We are building a college-level prototype for:

**SIH26047 — Patient Case-Taking Software**

Project name:

# MediKiosk

The core problem:

Indian hospital OPDs are overcrowded and doctors have very limited consultation time. A significant part of the consultation is spent collecting patient history and reviewing previous documents.

Our solution collects structured patient history **before the doctor consultation** and presents it to the doctor in a structured format.

# 2. OUR SELECTED SOLUTION

We decided to build three interfaces around the same workflow:

### 1. MediKiosk
For:
- elderly patients
- low-literacy patients
- patients uncomfortable with smartphones
- first-time digital users

The interface should have:
- large buttons
- simple language
- Hindi/English
- touch-friendly controls
- voice-style interaction
- minimal typing
- audio-friendly prompts

### 2. MediWeb
For:
- literate adults
- digitally comfortable users

It should provide the same basic clinical history workflow but with a normal modern web interface.

### 3. Doctor Portal
For doctors.

It should show:
- patient details
- chief complaint
- history
- symptoms
- previous medical information
- reports
- priority alerts
- structured clinical summary
- edit/confirm controls

# 16. CROSS-QUESTIONING PREPARATION

The judges may ask:

### "Why not just use a mobile app?"

Answer:

> "A mobile-only solution can exclude elderly, low-literacy and first-time users. MediKiosk provides an assisted entry point while MediWeb serves digitally comfortable users."

### "Is it diagnosing?"

> "No. It collects and structures clinical history. Priority alerts are based on predefined red-flag responses. Final diagnosis and clinical decisions remain with the doctor."

### "What if AI is wrong?"

> "The summary is a draft. The doctor can review, edit and confirm it."

### "Why no backend?"

> "The current prototype focuses on validating the patient-to-doctor workflow. Backend persistence and integration are the next implementation layer."

### "Is OCR real?"

> "The prototype demonstrates the workflow using sample extracted data. Production would connect a medical OCR pipeline."

### "Why only three diseases?"

> "We selected three representative pathways for the prototype. The architecture can be extended to additional clinical pathways."

### "What happens if the patient can't speak?"

> "Voice is optional. Every question can also be answered through touch."

### "What if the hospital is noisy?"

> "Touch remains available as a fallback. Production deployment can also use noise-robust speech recognition."

### "Are you replacing nurses?"

> "No. The system handles repetitive structured intake while clinical staff remain available for patients requiring assistance or clinical judgment."
